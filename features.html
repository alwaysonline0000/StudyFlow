What StudyFlow does

A plan that works with you.

StudyFlow combines task breakdown, deadline-aware study scheduling, and progress tracking in one calm workspace. This classroom prototype demonstrates the experience with a transparent rule-based planner.

✦

Natural-language task breakdown

Describe a midterm, essay, coding assignment, or presentation. Get relevant steps based on recognizable study keywords.

▦

Personalized daily sessions

Choose a deadline and daily study capacity. StudyFlow distributes the estimated workload across the available days.

↻

Adaptive rescheduling

Mark a session missed and generate a new schedule for unfinished work without losing completed progress.
